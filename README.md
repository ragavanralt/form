# Form

A matchday-style daily tracker for food, training, cardio and creatine. Each day is scored out of 10 and becomes a win, draw or loss.

Every month is a season with promotion and relegation between five divisions. Plan an international break for exam weeks, set a weekly wager, earn matchday packs, and add kick-off calls to your iPhone Calendar so the app reminds you to log.

Open the site on an iPhone in Safari, tap Share, then *Add to Home Screen*. It runs full screen from the home screen.

- Everything you log stays on your device (browser storage). Nothing is sent to a server.
- AI food estimates are optional and use your own free Gemini API key, which is stored only on your device.
- Export a backup from Card, then Export.
